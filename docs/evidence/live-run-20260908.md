## What this proves, and what it does not

- Proves: the installed binary completes a small real task on a free route end to end, with
  the plan ledger, evidence recording and the new per-completion composition working live.
- Proves: the number that matters for free tiers. **96% of every prompt is fixed overhead** —
  the doctrine (46%) and the tool schemas (50%) — and the conversation is 4%. On a route with no
  prompt caching every one of those 84 KB is read again on each of the 12 completions. That is
  the next economics target, and it is now measured rather than guessed.
- Does not prove: long-horizon reliability (this is a five-minute task), the reviewer recall's
  live hit rate, or the helper route (off by default; not exercised here).
