# When something goes wrong

What Rune says when something fails, what it does about it on its own, and what is left for
you. Seeded from the incident classes in `packages/shared/src/incident.ts`, which `rune
incidents` lists from the black box. Each row names the test that pins the behaviour, so a
change that breaks the row breaks a test first.

`rune doctor` checks the install, the session database and the provider routes without running
a task. For `rune -P` in scripts and CI, every way a run ends and its exit code are in
[`ci.md`](ci.md#how-a-run-ends).

## The provider

| What you see                                                                                             | What Rune does on its own                                                                                                                                                                 | What to do                                                                      | Pinned by                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "paused at the provider's usage limit -- it resets at HH:MM" (`quota_wall`, exit 5)                      | Stops at once, writes the handoff, and waits for the reset if `--resume-until` (or the TUI's auto-resume) is on                                                                           | Wait for `resumeAt`, or switch with `/model`                                    | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-quota-stop.test.ts`                                          |
| "All providers rate limited … Retry in ~Ns" (`provider.rate_limit_wait`)                                 | Waits out a short limit (twice per run, never past the outage deadline) and goes on                                                                                                       | Nothing, unless it repeats; then switch with `/model`                           | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-outage-deadline.test.ts`                                     |
| "Too many consecutive errors (N)" (`provider_lost`, exit 5)                                              | Re-sends within the gateway's retries and the run's error budget, all inside a 10-minute outage deadline, then hands off                                                                  | Try again later; `rune resume` continues from the handoff                       | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-provider-lost.test.ts`                                       |
| "The computer was asleep for X and the connection dropped — sending the request again once it is awake." | Does not count the failure; re-sends once the machine has been awake for 10 s (3 per wake, 50 per run). A system time change is not taken for a sleep                                     | Nothing                                                                         | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-slept.test.ts`, `tests/reliability/transport.test.ts`        |
| "The response was cut after N finished tool calls — running them and continuing."                        | Keeps the finished calls, drops the half-sent one, runs them, continues                                                                                                                   | Nothing                                                                         | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-stream-cut.test.ts`                                          |
| "Response interrupted mid-stream — restarting it."                                                       | A response cut before any call finished is sent again from zero, and what it showed is taken back. A stream from an OpenAI-compatible host that ends with no `finish_reason` is a cut too | Nothing                                                                         | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-stream-reset.test.ts`, `tests/reliability/transport.test.ts` |
| "stream stalled — no data for Ns"                                                                        | Gives up on a stream silent for 45 s between chunks (120 s before the first; 4 to 5 minutes for a model that thinks before it writes) and sends it again                                  | Nothing, unless it repeats; then switch with `/model`                           | `tests/reliability/transport.test.ts`                                                            |
| A 529 or 503 "Overloaded"                                                                                | Waits as long as the host's `Retry-After` asks, then sends it again                                                                                                                       | Nothing                                                                         | `tests/reliability/transport.test.ts`                                                            |
| "… rejected the request — … Check the model/key or switch with /model." (`provider.auth`)                | Stops; a key problem is not retried                                                                                                                                                       | Fix the key with `/login`, or switch with `/model`                              | `tests/unit/gateway/stream-usage-and-guard.test.ts`                                              |
| "No credits on <provider>." (`provider.no_credits`)                                                      | Stops                                                                                                                                                                                     | Add billing, or switch with `/model`                                            | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-quota-stop.test.ts`                                          |
| "The conversation no longer fits this model's context window …" (`max_tokens`, exit 4)                   | Compacts twice first; ends at once if that cannot make it fit, never re-sending the same prompt                                                                                           | Resume in a fresh session from the handoff, or use a model with a larger window | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-stream-reset.test.ts`                                        |

## Tools

| What you see                                                                                     | What Rune does on its own                                                                                                | What to do                                                             | Pinned by                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An edit refused: the text does not match, the file is not UTF-8, or it changed since it was read | Refuses rather than guess; names the nearest match; keeps line endings, the BOM and the file mode on every write         | Nothing; the model re-reads and retries                                | `tests/helpers/file-contract.ts` (unit and integration halves)  |
| "Still running after Ns, so it was moved to background shell …"                                  | An install, test suite, build or download that outlives its timeout keeps running; the model reads it with `bash_output` | Nothing; `kill_shell` stops it                                         | `tests/unit/tool-registry/background.test.ts`                   |
| "Stopped: it reached its time limit, not a kill_shell."                                          | A handed-over shell stops after 30 minutes, and in a headless run every background shell does                            | Start it with `run_in_background` if it is meant to run longer         | `tests/unit/tool-registry/background.test.ts`                   |
| "Command timed out after Nms"                                                                    | Any other command is stopped with its whole process group                                                                | Run it with `run_in_background`, or a larger `timeout_ms`              | `crates/rune-tools/src/bash.rs` (`timeout_kills_process_group`) |
| "… (sandboxed). If it needs network access …, re-run with network: true."                        | The sandbox refused; the hint names the wall                                                                             | Nothing; the model re-runs with `network: true` when the work needs it | `tests/unit/tool-registry/sandbox-launch.test.ts`               |
| "N permission request(s) denied — no approver in a headless run." (exit 3)                       | A headless run denies what it cannot ask about and keeps working                                                         | Pass `--auto-approve`, or set rules for what may run                   | `tests/unit/orchestrator/headless.test.ts`                      |

## Safety in Auto mode

| What you see                                                                                  | What Rune does on its own                                                                                       | What to do                                       | Pinned by                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `blocked` on an action                                                                        | The action does not run; the agent is told why and goes on                                                      | Nothing                                          | `tests/unit/orchestrator/auto-mode.test.ts`     |
| `held for you` — "Auto mode has blocked N actions in a row, so this one is held for the user" | After 3 blocks in a row or 20 in a run, the next is put on the end-of-turn list instead of asked about          | Run exactly that step from the list, or leave it | `tests/unit/orchestrator/auto-mode.test.ts`     |
| `run halted` (`halted`, exit 1)                                                               | Only when a tool result this session read was flagged as injected instructions; the agent is asked for a report | Read what it read before resuming                | `tests/integration/engine-halt-to-loop.test.ts` |

See [`auto-mode.md`](auto-mode.md#what-ends-a-run).

## Stopping and resuming

| What you see                                          | What Rune does on its own                                                                                                                                    | What to do                                  | Pinned by                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A run stopped with Ctrl+C or `kill` (exit 130 or 143) | Stops the command or the end-of-turn check it was running, with it; whether it was answering, running a tool, compacting or checking, the session is saved   | `rune resume`, or `rune -P … --resume <id>` | `tests/reliability/process.test.ts`                                                                                             |
| A run killed outright: `kill -9`, a crash, the power  | The transcript is written as the run goes. A tool's command, the end-of-turn check, hooks and background shells each stop within a second of their run dying | `rune resume`                               | `tests/integration/lifecycle-durability.test.ts`, `tests/reliability/process.test.ts`, `tests/unit/shared/parent-watch.test.ts` |

## Context and the loop

| What you see                                                                                      | What Rune does on its own                                                                                    | What to do                                                                                 | Pinned by                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Context window exceeded — compacted the conversation and retrying."                              | Compacts the working set and sends the turn again (twice per run)                                            | Nothing                                                                                    | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-stream-reset.test.ts`      |
| "The context fills up again within 2 turns of being compacted …" (`max_tokens`, exit 4)           | Stops compacting after three quick refills and ends the run                                                  | Read large files in pages, narrow searches and output, or hand large output to a sub-agent | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-compaction-thrash.test.ts` |
| "Infinite loop detected: same tool calls repeated without progress, even after a nudge." (exit 1) | Nudges once, then ends the run (`loop_detected`)                                                             | Resume with a different approach                                                           | `tests/unit/orchestrator/headless.test.ts`                     |
| "Stopped after N turns in which every tool call was refused before it ran." (exit 1)              | Nudges once, then ends the run (`barren`)                                                                    | Check what is being refused: permissions, the sandbox, or Auto mode                        | `tests/unit/orchestrator/agent-loop-halt.test.ts`              |
| "The run hit its turn ceiling without finishing" (`max_turns`, exit 4, headless only)             | A headless run stops at 80 turns, or `--max-turns`; an interactive session has no ceiling unless you set one | `rune resume`, or a larger `--max-turns`                                                   | `tests/unit/orchestrator/reliability-turns.test.ts`            |

## Saving and state

| What you see                                                                        | What Rune does on its own                                                                                   | What to do                                                               | Pinned by                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Recent messages may not be saved for resume: the session database refused a write" | Says it when writes start failing, again at the end of every turn while they do, and once when they recover | Free disk space, or make `~/.rune` writable                              | `tests/unit/orchestrator/engine-transcript-health.test.ts` |

## Install and upgrade

| What you see                                                 | What Rune does on its own                                              | What to do                                                     | Pinned by                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| "the download was cut after X MB; resuming (attempt N of 6)" | Asks for the rest of the file; verifies the whole against `SHA256SUMS` | Nothing                                                        | `tests/unit/orchestrator/upgrade-cli.test.ts`    |

## Recorded, not shown

Most incident classes are the harness watching itself: nudges, gates, effort routing, refunds.
They are in the black box for `rune incidents` and the retro, and never interrupt a run. The
classes this page covers are the ones a person can act on.
